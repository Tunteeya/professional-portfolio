Blockchain Technology

My journey to earning to Earing the Web3 Fundamentals Certificate at Hackerboost

October , 2024 | Umar Farouk



Over the past few years, the global technology landscape has been experiencing a tremendous shift—one that is steering us from the centralized systems we have always known into a decentralized future powered by blockchain technology. As a BSc Software Engineering student, I have always believed in staying ahead of technological innovations. That is why I decided to challenge myself by joining the Hackerboost Web3 Blockchain Bootcamp, a rigorous two-month training that ran from October to January.

Looking back, I can confidently say that this journey was more than just an academic exercise. It was a life-changing experience that equipped me with industry-relevant skills, connected me with brilliant instructors and peers, and gave me a fresh perspective on how Web3 technologies are shaping the future of software engineering.

The Beginning of My Web3 Journey

When I first enrolled in the Web3 Fundamentals Certificate program, I was excited but also slightly unsure of what to expect. Blockchain was not entirely new to me—I had read about Bitcoin, Ethereum, and decentralized applications (dApps)—but I wanted to move beyond the headlines and dive deep into the technical core of Web3.

The bootcamp began in October, and from the very first session, I could feel the difference. The learning environment was highly practical, focused on building, experimenting, and problem-solving rather than just memorizing concepts. My instructors—Habib Maltiba, Likem Dzokoto, and Sahabia Yakubu—brought a wealth of knowledge and experience into the classroom. They not only explained the concepts but also guided us on how to apply them in real-world scenarios.

The Structure of the Bootcamp

The Hackerboost program was designed to progressively take us from fundamental concepts into advanced blockchain development. By the time the bootcamp ended in January, I had not only earned the Web3 Fundamentals Certificate, but also achieved two other important certifications, which are Web3 Developer Bootcamp Certificate and Web3 Developer Bootcamp Certificate.

This progression was crucial. We started with the basics: understanding blockchain as a distributed ledger, cryptographic principles, consensus mechanisms, and tokenomics. From there, we moved into hands-on projects involving smart contracts, decentralized applications (dApps), and Solidity programming.

Each stage of the bootcamp felt like a building block that seamlessly prepared me for the next, and by the end, I could confidently design and implement blockchain-based solutions.

Reflections on the Experience

As a Software Engineering student, the bootcamp was not only a valuable addition to my resume but also a strong complement to my academic field. Traditional software engineering often focuses on centralized systems—databases, servers, APIs—but the Web3 space challenges these assumptions by introducing decentralization, trustless systems, and peer-to-peer networks.

Through this bootcamp, I was able to connect the dots between what I study in class and the future of software systems:

Software Architecture vs. Smart Contracts : In my degree program, I learn about modular design and system architecture. Solidity programming gave me the opportunity to apply these principles in writing smart contracts that are both efficient and secure.

Databases vs. Blockchain Ledgers : Instead of relational databases, blockchain offers a decentralized, immutable ledger. Understanding the technical workings of consensus mechanisms has reshaped how I think about data integrity and security.

Cybersecurity and Trust : Blockchain's cryptographic foundations tie in perfectly with my coursework in security, but the bootcamp emphasized real-world applications of encryption and digital signatures.

Agile Development in Web3 : Just like in software engineering, agile principles apply in blockchain development. Iterative testing of smart contracts and dApps taught me how crucial debugging, peer review, and security audits are in this field.

The Human Element: Instructors and Community

What stood out most in this journey was the mentorship and collaboration. Instructors like Habib Maltiba, Likem Dzokoto, and Sahabia Yakubu were not just teachers—they were industry practitioners. They shared not only technical skills but also career advice, insights into industry trends, and the importance of building a strong developer community.

Working alongside other participants was equally rewarding. We shared challenges, brainstormed solutions, and celebrated wins together. This collaborative spirit mirrors the open-source culture of Web3 itself, where developers across the world collectively build the future.

How This Experience Shapes My Future

Now, as I continue my BSc in Software Engineering, I feel more prepared than ever to align my career with the opportunities emerging in the Web3 space. The certificates I earned are not just pieces of paper—they represent hours of dedication, problem-solving, and skill acquisition.

This experience has motivated me to:

Specialize in Blockchain Development : I plan to integrate my Web3 and Solidity skills into future projects and academic research.

Contribute to Open Source : Inspired by the Web3 community, I want to actively contribute to decentralized projects that impact people globally.

Bridge Web2 and Web3 : With my background in software engineering, I see myself helping traditional industries transition into decentralized models, creating hybrid solutions that merge both worlds.
