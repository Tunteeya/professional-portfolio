My journey to earning to Earing the Web3 Fundamentals Certificate at Hackerboost

October , 2024 | Umar Farouk



In September 2025 , Tamale became a hub of creativity and innovation as students and developers from across the region gathered for a three-day HackerBoost Workshop. The event, which ran from Friday through Sunday, offered an exciting opportunity to explore cutting-edge technologies and dive deep into the future of Web3 development.

A Weekend of Learning and Collaboration

The workshop was organized as part of the Sui Ghana community’s ongoing efforts to build capacity among young developers and enthusiasts in the blockchain ecosystem. The sessions were hands-on, engaging, and insightful — bridging theory and real-world application.

Participants were introduced to Move, the programming language behind the Sui blockchain. The facilitators guided everyone through the installation process using Chocolatey, the Command Line Interface (CLI), and other supporting tools. For many attendees, it was their first encounter with the Move language, making it a valuable and eye-opening experience.

Beyond blockchain, the workshop also covered essential topics in React and Node.js, giving participants a broader understanding of modern web development. The combination of backend and frontend technologies provided a well-rounded foundation for building decentralized applications (dApps) and web-based platforms.

Empowering the Next Generation of Developers

A key highlight of the workshop was the presence of Mrs. Uju, the only female among the Sui Ghana team. Her contribution stood out as a reminder of the importance of inclusivity and women’s representation in the tech ecosystem. Her mentorship and words of encouragement inspired many, especially young women aspiring to enter the tech industry.

Throughout the three days, the energy in the room was electric — filled with collaboration, curiosity, and creativity. Students exchanged ideas, engaged in discussions, and connected with developers and mentors who shared their passion for innovation. The event went beyond coding — it built a sense of community and shared purpose.

Looking Ahead

By the end of the weekend, participants walked away with new skills, valuable insights, and stronger professional networks. The HackerBoost Workshop in Tamale not only strengthened local technical capacity but also reinforced Ghana’s growing presence in the global Web3 and blockchain space.
